Transmission now runs on Windows: the download page has a 64-bit Windows installer beside the two Mac dmgs. Transmission is also open source now, under the MIT license, and its documentation and downloads have moved to github.com/factus10/transmission. On a Mac, the app has a new bundle ID, so the first Miro export after updating may ask to use your saved token (choose Always Allow), and if double-clicking a project opens an older copy of the app, delete that copy from Applications.
