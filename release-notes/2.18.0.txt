Reports now count content pages rather than crawled URLs — inherited redirects and dead links were being counted as pages to migrate, overstating both your inventory and the share recommended for removal. If you have already sent a client an Executive Summary, it is worth regenerating. Inherited 301s also keep their destinations current, re-deriving on every save instead of freezing at the old site's answer, so a redirect follows its target when that page moves or is consolidated. Also: permanent row deletion, greater-than and less-than field filters, and fixes to filter chips that could not be dismissed and the Triage Non-200 dialog.
